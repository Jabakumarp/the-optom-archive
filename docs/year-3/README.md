# Year 3 · Advanced Clinical Optometry & Specialties

### Folders:
- `docs/year-3/binocular-vision/`: Grades of BSV, Correspondence, Strabismus diagnosis (Hirschberg, Krimsky, Cover Test, PBCT), Amblyopia diagnosis & therapy, AC/A ratio, Non-strabismic binocular anomalies (CI, CE, DI, DE), Orthoptic therapy.
- `docs/year-3/contact-lens/`: Corneal topography, soft spherical & toric contact lens fitting, RGP fitting & fluorescein pattern evaluation, care systems, complication management, specialty lenses (Keratoconus, Scleral lenses, Orthokeratology).
- `docs/year-3/low-vision-aids/`: Low vision assessment, visual impairment classifications, optical aids (microscopes, telescopes, stand magnifiers, hand magnifiers), non-optical aids, electronic magnifiers, rehabilitation strategies.
- `docs/year-3/glaucoma-investigative-optometry/`: Glaucoma pathophysiology, tonometry (GAT, NCT), gonioscopy (Shaffer, Spaeth), automated visual fields (Humphrey SITA), OCT imaging (RNFL, Ganglion Cell Analysis), anterior segment imaging.
- `docs/year-3/dispensing-optics/`: Frame materials, frame measurements, face measurements (PD, pupil height, vertex distance, pantoscopic angle), progressive addition lenses (PALs), occupational lenses, lab surfacing and edging.
- `docs/year-3/paediatric-geriatric-optometry/`: Paediatric refraction, visual development, VA testing methods in infants/children, geriatric visual changes, presbyopic management, cognitive factors.
- `docs/year-3/occupational-public-health-optometry/`: Industrial eye safety, standards, visual ergonomic audits, computer vision syndrome (CVS), community screening, RAAB survey, NPCBVI programs.
