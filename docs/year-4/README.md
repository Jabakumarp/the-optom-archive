# Year 4 · Clinical Internship & Applied Clinical Research

### Folders:
- `docs/year-4/clinical-protocols/`: Comprehensive routine eye examination protocols, specialty clinic manuals (Cornea, Glaucoma, Retina, Pediatric, Low Vision, Contact Lens), emergency triage and referral guidelines.

- `docs/year-4/research-projects/`: Research methodology, study designs (Cross-sectional, Cohort, RCT), ethical clearance templates, biostatistics guide, thesis & presentation formatting.
