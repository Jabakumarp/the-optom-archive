# Year 2 · Optometric Optics & Visual Optics

### Folders:
- `docs/year-2/optometric-optics/`: Spectacle lens materials (CR-39, Polycarbonate, High-Index), Abbe value, surfacing, base curves, Prentice rule, transposition, tinting & AR coatings.
- `docs/year-2/visual-optics/`: Optics of ametropia (Myopia, Hyperopia, Astigmatism, Presbyopia), retinal image size, schematic eye (Gullstrand, Emsley), accommodation, depth of focus, streak retinoscopy, and subjective refraction.
- `docs/year-2/ocular-diseases/`: Diseases of conjunctiva, cornea, uvea, sclera, lens (cataract), retina (diabetic retinopathy, vascular occlusions), and optic nerve anomalies.
- `docs/year-2/ocular-pharmacology/`: Routes of administration, diagnostic agents (Fluorescein, Rose Bengal), mydriatics/cycloplegics (Tropicamide, Atropine), anti-glaucoma drugs, antibiotics, anti-inflammatory agents.
- `docs/year-2/pathology-microbiology/`: General pathology, inflammation, wound healing, ocular microbiology, bacterial/fungal/viral keratitis, diagnostic smears and cultures.
